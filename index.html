01 / ABOUT

Curious about the why behind the results.

I enjoy using mathematics, statistics, and computation to make sense of complex real-world problems.

My academic path spans Physics at the University of Yaoundé I, Data Science studies at École Nationale Supérieure Polytechnique de Douala, and the AIMS Master’s in Mathematical Sciences.

My research project, “Clustering Epidemic Trajectories Using Functional Data Analysis Techniques,” strengthened my interest in methods for understanding temporal and epidemiological data. I’m especially interested in moving from descriptive analysis toward predictive, interpretable tools that can support decisions.
