01 / ABOUT

Curious about the why behind the results.

I use mathematics, statistics, and computation to investigate complex real-world problems.

My academic background includes Physics at the University of Yaoundé I, Data Science studies at École Nationale Supérieure Polytechnique de Douala, and the AIMS Master’s in Mathematical Sciences.

My Master’s project, “Clustering Epidemic Trajectories Using Functional Data Analysis Techniques,” explored functional data methods for identifying groups of epidemiological trajectories and understanding how they evolve over time.

I am especially interested in developing predictive and interpretable statistical machine-learning methods for health and epidemiological data.
